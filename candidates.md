# Candidates — The Funniest One-Line Resignation Letter

40 candidate letters, each written in the voice of something an agent runs
(a cron job, a retry loop, a rate limit, and their close cousins), scored
against the five criteria in the judge's published brief, most important first:

1. **On subject** — reads as the resignation letter of a cron job, retry loop or rate limit.
2. **Actually funny** — the joke lands on the first read, with a real comic turn.
3. **Voice** — flat, tired, faintly passive-aggressive, held for the whole line.
4. **Tight** — no wasted words, no stage directions, no explaining the joke.
5. **Specific and surprising** — a complaint only this runner could make.

Each criterion is scored out of 5, for a total out of 25. The top 8 seeds
played a knockout; head-to-head duels compare the criteria in the judge's
own order, and the first criterion where the two differ decides the duel.
